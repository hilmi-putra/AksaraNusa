# Aksara Nusa Mediatama - Context & Design Guidelines

## Project Focus
- **Brand Transition**: This project is transitioning away from "Mega Press" to **Aksara Nusa Mediatama**. All styling, wording, and assets must reflect the new brand identity.
- **Content-First Approach**: Focus on **copywriting** and typography. 
- **Premium Aesthetics**: The UI must look like a high-end, premium publisher. 
- **No AI Slop**: Do NOT use generic AI UI tropes (excessive blurs, random floating orbs, glowing gradients everywhere, or large unnecessary decorative image assets). Keep it clean, structured, and professional.

## Color Palette
Use the following brand colors for styling:
- **Primary (Blue)**: `#004A8F` and deeper shades like `#002D5A`.
- **Secondary/Accent (Orange)**: `#EF7A08` and its variants for CTA and accents.
- **Neutrals**: White, off-white (`#F8FAFC`), and Slate for text (`#171512` or `slate-900`).

## UI/UX Rules
1. **Typography is King**: Use elegant typography (Serifs for headings, clean Sans-serifs for body) and let the text breathe with good whitespace.
2. **Layouts**: Prefer clean edge-to-edge designs or properly aligned structured layouts (centered or grid-based) over messy floating elements.
3. **Assets**: Use minimal, aesthetic CSS-based elements or small high-quality icons rather than massive AI-generated image blocks or unreliable external image links.
4. **Copywriting**: Ensure wording is inspiring, professional, and tailored for authors and book lovers (Penerbit indie, naskah inspiratif, hak cipta).
